Methodology

The composite score is a z-scored blend of momentum and quality components, computed over a trailing 12-month window:

ComponentWeightDefinition
Momentum (12-1)50%Return from t-252 to t-22 (skips the last month to avoid short-term reversal)
Quality (Sharpe)30%Annualized mean daily return / annualized daily vol, trailing 252 days
Low Volatility10%Inverse z-score of annualized realized vol
Low Drawdown10%Inverse z-score of max drawdown from peak

All components are z-scored across the universe before weighting. A higher composite score indicates stronger momentum and better risk-adjusted performance.

Universe: 19 liquid large-cap US names. Data: Yahoo Finance adjusted closes, trailing ~3 years. Cadence: re-run monthly; each run produces a new dated CSV and report.
